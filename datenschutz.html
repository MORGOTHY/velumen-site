2. Kurzfassung

  • Velumen braucht kein Konto, keine E-Mail-Adresse und keinen echten Namen.
  • Die App enthält keine Werbung und kein Tracking; es werden keine Werbe-Identifikatoren verwendet und keine Daten an Werbenetzwerke weitergegeben.

6. Kinder

Velumen richtet sich nicht gezielt an Kinder unter 13 Jahren und erhebt wissentlich keine personenbezogenen Daten von Kindern. Der Spielername wird von den Spielenden selbst gewählt; bitte keinen echten Namen verwenden.

Du kannst Auskunft über die zu deiner anonymen Nutzer-ID gespeicherten Daten verlangen sowie deren Berichtigung oder Löschung. Privacy Policy Velumen

Last updated: September 2026 · Applies to the iOS app “Velumen”

Who we are

App provider: Kevin Ramel, Switzerland. Contact: kevinramel190@gmail.com.

In short

  • Velumen needs no account, no e-mail address and no real name.
  • The app contains no ads and no tracking; no advertising identifiers are used and nothing is shared with ad networks.
  • Your save game is stored on your device.
  • Leaderboards and Cloud Sync use an anonymous, randomly generated user ID that cannot be linked to a person.

What we process

  • Save game (Lumen, skins, achievements, settings, player name) – stored only on your device until you delete the app.
  • Anonymous user ID – created by Firebase Authentication so that only you can update your own leaderboard entry.
  • Leaderboard entry (chosen player name, depth reached, timestamp) – stored in Cloud Firestore (Google), data centre Zurich, Switzerland; boards reset weekly / daily.
  • Cloud Sync code with save data – only when you create a code yourself; valid for 10 minutes, then unusable; entries are deleted periodically.
  • In-app purchases – handled entirely by Apple; the app only receives the purchase confirmation, never payment details.

When online features are used, your device technically transmits its IP address to Google (Firebase), used solely to provide the service and prevent abuse.

Service providers

Google Ireland Ltd. (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore – privacy) and Apple Inc. (App Store, in-app purchases – privacy).

Legal basis

Processing is necessary to provide the game and its online features (Swiss Data Protection Act; Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where applicable). Online features are optional; the game also works offline.

Children

Velumen is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Player names are chosen by the player; please do not use a real name.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of the data stored under your anonymous user ID. Because we cannot identify you, please include your player name. Delete your local save by deleting the app or resetting progress in the settings. Contact: kevinramel190@gmail.com.
